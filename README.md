# WanderNest – AI Travel Agency Platform
WanderNest is a modern AI-powered travel planning platform designed to simplify and enhance the entire travel experience. From destination discovery to detailed itineraries and budgeting tools, WanderNest helps travelers plan smarter, faster, and with more confidence.
## Overview
WanderNest helps users:

Get AI-generated destination suggestions

Create interactive, multi-step travel plans

Manage trips from a central dashboard

Explore detailed destination insights

Organize travel budgets, packing lists, and trip notes

Built to be fast, intuitive, and visually stunning.

## Features

### Core Features

AI-Powered Trip Planning
Generate smart travel recommendations based on preferences.

Interactive Trip Creation
Step-by-step trip planner with progress tracking.

Trip Management Dashboard
View, update, and manage all your trips.

Destination Insights
Includes climate, best time to visit, cost, activities, and more.

Budget Planning
Get cost estimates based on travel duration and lifestyle.

Smart Packing Checklist
Auto-generated packing list for each destination.

### Destination Coverage

WanderNest includes real destination metadata for:

Chicago, USA – Architecture, city life

Santorini, Greece – Romantic islands

Bali, Indonesia – Tropical nature & culture

Kyoto, Japan – Temples, traditions, history

Paris, France – Romance & iconic attractions

Istanbul, Turkey – Cross-continent cultural blend
